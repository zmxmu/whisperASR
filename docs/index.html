Live transcription & translation

Transcribe any audio.
Translate it instantly.

Real-time transcription and translation of any app's audio — powered by whisper.cpp on your Mac's Metal GPU. Completely private, fully on-device. And when the meeting ends, one click turns the transcript into meeting minutes.

Record any app's audio system-wide — Zoom, YouTube, podcasts — and watch the transcript appear word by word with simultaneous translation flowing alongside it.

File
transcription

Drag & drop any audio or video file. WhisperASR transcribes it with a live progress bar and time estimate. MP3, M4A, MP4, MOV and more.

🌐

Bilingual
output

Every segment shows the original transcript alongside your translation in teal. Export the transcript to a text file when done.

📋

Meeting minutes
in one click

Pick a prompt from the transcript toolbar and WhisperASR turns the whole transcript into structured minutes — summary, decisions, action items — rendered as clean HTML. Copy them fully formatted into Mail, Word or Google Docs, or export as a file.

Custom prompt templates for different meetings
Long recordings are chunked and summarized automatically
Copy as rich text or export HTML

Download any of seven speech recognition models right in the app — from the 78 MB Whisper Tiny to Breeze-ASR-25, tuned for Mandarin and Taiwanese-accented speech — and switch between them anytime.

Fully local inference

Whisper runs on-device via whisper.cpp. Your audio and transcripts never touch a server.

Leverages Apple's Metal framework for fast transcription on M-series chips.
